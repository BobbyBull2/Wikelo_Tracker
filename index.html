Material You Have
  • ASD Secure Drive
    Farm via investigation contracts at ASD Onyx Facilities (Hurston & microTech bunkers). Fastest method: engineering wing speedruns (1–2 drives in 5–10 min). Also common in Jumptown events and 890 Jump wrecks.
  • Carinite / Carinite (Pure)
    Mine raw Carinite on Aberdeen, Lyria, or Aaron Halo asteroid clusters. Pure variant exposed in craters during Hathor Orbital Laser events ("Align and Mine"). Refine quickly at L1 orbital decks.
  • Council Scrip
    Earn from unverified council missions (headhunter/ambush) on Stanton planets (15 scrip each). Foxwell for MG scrip, other moons for Council. High-rep farming yields 12–15/hour.
  • DCHS-05 Orbital Positioning Comp-Board
    Contested Zones: Ruin Station vault (green key), Checkmate (red doors), Orbituary Hangar 30. Duplicate at printers for multiples; AFK farm green rooms safely.
  • Government Cartography Agency Medal (Pristine)
    Best from ace pilots in orange-level patrol contracts (Foxwell ~25% chance). Also random loot in Hurston/microTech derelict outposts and blue hangars/red rooms. Also sometimes in OLP loot rooms and NYX QV Warehouse.
  • Irradiated Valakkar Pearl (AAA/AA) & Fang (Apex/Adult/Juvenile)
    Valakkar worms in Lazarus (Pyro I) during Storm Breaker events. AAA/Apex from boss variants; adults easier for groups. Bring heavy weapons; farm during event windows.
  • Jaclium (Ore)
    Surface mining on Daymar/Yela or Aaron Halo asteroid belts. Use ROC or Prospector; look for high-density clusters. Refine at L1 orbital stations. Hand mine in craters during Hathor Orbital Laser events ("Align and Mine").
  • Large Artefact Fragment (Pristine)
    Loot from derelict outposts (Hurston/microTech), QV Station warehouses (blue boxes). 1 fragment per 5 warehouses; also salvage missions or random crates.
  • MG Scrip
    Verified mercenary contracts (Foxwell ambush/patrol, 15 scrip each), Gilly's Flight School missions. High-rep farms 132–165/hour. Trade 50 for 1 Wikelo Favor.
  • NN-13 Cannon
    Orbituary Hangar 30, Onyx Facilities; duplicate at printers. Also Wikelo Golem contracts or contested zones (6–7 needed for IKTI mods).
  • Polaris Bit
    Trade 24 SCU Quantainium via Wikelo’s "Want Polaris? Need something special" contract. Farm high-yield clusters in Aaron Halo belt.
  • Quantainium
    Aaron Halo asteroid belt (best clusters), Lyria caves, Aberdeen surface. Use Prospector; refine within 50 min before instability.
  • RCMBNT-RGL-1 / -2 / -3
    Jorrit Dossier: Project Hyperion in ASD Onyx Facilities. RGL from gravity labs, XTL from bio processors. Choose mission variants for specific drops.
  • Tevarin War Service Marker (Pristine)
    Ace pilots in orange patrol contracts (~25% chance). Also Hurston/microTech derelict outposts and blue hangars/red rooms. Also sometimes in OLP loot rooms and NYX QV Warehouse.
  • Tundra Kopion Horn
    Hunt Tundra Kopions on microTech (frozen biomes near New Babbage, e.g., MT Datacenter KH3 at 231°, 36.5km). Pack hunting yields best results, look for snow meets forest landscape.
  • UEE 6th Platoon Medal (Pristine)
    Ace pilots in orange patrol contracts (Foxwell ~25%). Also Hurston/microTech derelict blue hangars/red rooms and random loot. Also sometimes in OLP loot rooms and NYX QV Warehouse.
  • Vanduul Metal / Plating
    Salvage Vanduul wrecks in Pyro (higher drop from Scythe/Blade ships). Also Nyx Vanduul-Tech missions (Auxilia station yields ~8).
  • Yormandi Eye / Tongue
    Yormandi boss in ASD Onyx Facilities (Site B research wing). Kill and extract after fight; trade with Wikelo for binoculars or other rewards.

Tips updated for 4.0+. Check starcitizen.tools/Wikelo or community Discords for latest routes.
